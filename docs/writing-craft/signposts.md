## The reader moves, not the text

- Before: "The previous section showed that the budget depends on the new HELOC." After: "As we saw, the budget depends on the new HELOC." — invented; Pinker, Ch. 2, PDF pp. 46–48. _From Pinker; not yet seen in your writing._
- The academic sheet's purpose statement ("This report is to inform…") overrides it in academic work. The Voice sheet's "This section identifies…" and "No preview list" lines overlap; #321's audit decides whether they move.
