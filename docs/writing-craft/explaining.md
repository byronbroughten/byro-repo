# Explaining: examples

Disclosed from [`docs/writing-craft.md`](../writing-craft.md), "Explaining". The rules are there, one line each; this file holds each rule's before-and-after and where in Pinker's _The Sense of Style_ it comes from. An instance marked _from Pinker; not yet seen in your writing_ is invented, and a real one replaces it when it turns up.

## A comparison that works the same way

- Before: "Home equity is like a savings account you can draw on." After: "A HELOC is like a credit card whose limit grows as you pay down the mortgage." — invented; a savings account isn't borrowed against, so it misleads on the one point that matters. Pinker, Ch. 2, PDF pp. 40–43. _From Pinker; not yet seen in your writing._
- It sharpens the Voice sheet's "Explain an unfamiliar idea by comparing it to something the reader already knows"; #321's audit decides whether that line moves.

## A second example to fix the feature

- Before: "A fixed cost, such as the mortgage payment, doesn't change with occupancy." After: "A fixed cost, such as the mortgage payment or the MLS subscription, doesn't change with occupancy." — invented; the second rules out "it's fixed because it's a loan". Pinker, Ch. 3, PDF pp. 72–73. _From Pinker; not yet seen in your writing._

## No apology for the topic

- Before: "Estimating rent for an owner-occupied unit is inherently complex." After: "The owner's unit is priced at the rent a comparable unit leases for." — invented; Pinker, Ch. 2, PDF pp. 49–50. _From Pinker; not yet seen in your writing._
