# Writing clarity

Rules any skilled writer follows, whatever their voice, so a reader can parse and follow reader-facing prose ([defined in `prose-files.md`](./agents/prose-files.md#terms)); it sits beside the Voice sheet, [`writing-voice.md`](./writing-voice.md), and academic work layers [`academic-writing-style.md`](./academic-writing-style.md) on both.

A rule belongs here when a good writer with a different voice would still follow it.

One line per rule. The examples and their sources are one file away. Open a reasoning file only when you're changing the rule, or the rule's line doesn't decide your case.

## Terms

- **Garden path**: a sentence whose first words invite one parse that a later word overturns, so the reader has to back up and reread.
- **Curse of knowledge**: the writer's difficulty imagining what the reader doesn't already know, which leaves terms undefined and steps skipped.
- **Given before new**: the order that puts what the reader already knows early in a sentence and the new information at its end.

## Precedence

- **A Clarity rule wins over a Voice habit only when the habit makes the reader misread or reread a sentence; matters of taste stay with the habit.**
