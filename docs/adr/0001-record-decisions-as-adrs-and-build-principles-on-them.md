# Record decisions as Matt Pocock's ADRs, and build design principles on top of them

The repo had forked away from Matt Pocock's ADRs, recording a decision only as a design.md principle instance or a parked candidate, which interprets the decision rather than stating it and drifts from its issue. Decisions are now recorded as his ADRs, in his format plus a required `Source:` line, in one `docs/adr/` per repo, and each design principle cites at least two of them. A short ADR that points at its issue, with a backlink from that issue, answers the worry that a second filing system would drift from the issues.

Source: byronbroughten/byro-repo#369
