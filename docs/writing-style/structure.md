# Structure and recommendations: instances

Disclosed from [`docs/writing-style.md`](../writing-style.md), "Structure and recommendations". The rules are there, one line each; this file holds the quoted instances and their sources. "Seen in" counts the reading batches, out of 6, that reported the habit ([sources](./sources.md)). "In N of 5 posts" counts the blog posts.

## Hook, then scope

- "First time home buyers pay on average about $22,063 at or around the time of purchase. And that's assuming there are no upfront repair costs. In this article we'll break down the cost of buying a house and explore ways to lower it." — blog: The Cost of Buying a House
- "90% of homeowners underestimate the costs of owning a home, and 73% have regrets about their home purchase." — blog: Costs of Owning a Home
- "Owning a home isn't cheap." — blog: Home Cost Comparison
- In 5 of 5 posts. A one-sentence scope is kept; the multi-step "I will… I will then…" roadmap stays dropped ([sources](./sources.md)).

## Fence the scope

- "Note that this article is about the costs of homeownership rather than the upfront cash needed to buy a home in the first place." — blog: Costs of Owning a Home
- "Also, note that this article is intended to cover only costs at or around the time of purchase." — blog: The Cost of Buying a House
- "Throughout this article we'll be speaking in terms of long-term investment, basing our assessment in historical averages rather than short-term speculation." — blog: Is Buying a House a Good Investment?
- In 3 of 5 posts.

## Bullets for short parallel items

- "Savable and reusable deal components" (a slide bullet whose reasoning is in the notes) — DealLab - Brand Presentation
