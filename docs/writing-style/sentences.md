## Plain connectives

- "albeit with a proportional increase in cost of sales." — Assessment 2 - Capital Budgeting Tools
- "Additionally", "Finally", "Moreover" and "Furthermore" were seen in 3 of 6 batches; "Conversely", "albeit" and "To that end" in 1.
- The developer ruled in review that "Furthermore" and "Moreover" are academic, though the DealLab deck uses them ("Moreover, DealCheck is in an advantageous market position."), and that "Conversely", "albeit" and "To that end" belong to the voice, though the candidates first tagged them academic.
