# Numbers and evidence: instances

Disclosed from [`docs/writing-style.md`](../writing-style.md), "Numbers and evidence". The rules are there, one line each; this file holds the quoted instances and their sources. "Seen in" counts the reading batches, out of 6, that reported the habit ([sources](./sources.md)). "In N of 5 posts" counts the blog posts.

## Say what a figure means

- "This would equate to about $40,000 per year in revenue" — DealLab - Brand Presentation
- Seen in 5 of 6. The usual openers are "This means…", "This would equate to…" and "meaning…".
