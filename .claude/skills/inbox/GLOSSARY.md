# Inbox

The words of an `/inbox` session: Claude organizing a Gmail mailbox with the owner, where nothing changes without the owner's yes.

## Language

**Proposal**:
One concrete change Claude has drafted but not applied: a filter, a bulk label, archive or Trash pass, a label rename or delete, or an unsubscribe pick, stated with what it matches, what it does and how many existing messages it would catch.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, recommendation

**Sign-off**:
The owner's explicit yes to one specific Proposal, given in a session.
_Avoid_: Approval, consent

**Trash**:
Moving mail to Gmail's Trash, where it stays recoverable for 30 days; the strongest removal a Proposal can make.
_Avoid_: Delete

**Inbox**:
The mail that needs the owner's action, reply or at least a look; everything else is labeled and archived.
