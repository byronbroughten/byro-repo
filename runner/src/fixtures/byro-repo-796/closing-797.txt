A ticket's own `code` or `piece` label now picks its Workflow, falling back to its spec's label. In a spec labelled both, a ticket with neither label picks none.

- **The pick** (`runner/src/Workflow.ts`): `ticketWorkflowNameOf` and `ticketWorkflowOf` take `{ ticket, spec }` (the renamed `SessionSkillInput`, now `SpecTicket`). They return `undefined` for an unlabelled ticket of a spec labelled both. `pickedWorkflowOf` throws in that case, naming the ticket and the missing label. The words come from `unpickedWorkflowOf`, which the throw and the Halt share. `ticketWorkflowsOf` gives each Workflow a spec's tickets pick, once and in name order, leaving out a ticket that picks none. `workflowNameOf` and `workflowOf` stay for their callers until #800. The header comment now says a ticket's label picks its Workflow, falling back to the spec's.
- **Session skill**: `sessionSkillOf` now uses `pickedWorkflowOf`, so `IssueReader.sessionSkillOf` and `issue:start` throw on an unlabelled ticket of a spec labelled both.
- **Base forms** (`SpecRunBase`): `_ticketWorkflowOf(spec, ticket)` and `_ticketAgentStepOf(spec, ticket, name)` throw through `pickedWorkflowOf`. `_ticketWorkflowsOf(run)` reads the run's tickets and returns `ticketWorkflowsOf`, for #800's spec-end Steps. `_workflowOf` and `_agentStepOf` are unchanged.
- **The Halt**: `TicketSequencer._runTicket` checks the pick right after the untrusted-body check. When the ticket picks no Workflow, `HaltRaiser._unpickedWorkflowHalt` raises and posts a `spec-ambiguity` Halt on that ticket, built by `halts.ts`'s `unpickedWorkflowHaltOf`, and launches no step. I used the existing `spec-ambiguity` kind rather than adding a new one. Any answer but `stop spec` re-reads the labels at the next pick: a ticket that is still unlabelled gets a fresh Halt.
- **Tests**: `Workflow.test.ts` covers the pick (each criterion case, plus the throw and `ticketWorkflowsOf`). `IssueReader.test.ts` adds a `piece` ticket of a `code` spec getting `implement-piece`, and the throw. `SpecRun.halts.test.ts` adds the Halt and its re-read on an answer. One IssueReader test, "names /implement-code ... whatever its own labels", stated the old rule; it now tests a ticket with neither label. No spec-run test changed.
